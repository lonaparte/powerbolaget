Island climate calibration:
NASA POWER Climatology API (community RE), 2001-2025 gridded monthly
climatology at 57.5 N, 18.5 E. Parameters WS10M, WS50M, T2M,
ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN, RH2M. Source, period and retrieval date are stored in
app/island-climatology.json. Wind variability, persistence and the wind rose
are fitted to NASA POWER hourly data (WS10M, WD10M, T2M, 2020-2024) at the same
point; the daily temperature range follows Visby station averages published at
https://www.weatherandclimate.eu/climate/02590. Only summary statistics are
stored, in app/weather.py.

Island wind field: Global Wind Atlas, mean wind speed at 100 m (country GeoTIFF
for Sweden), averaged over 1 km cells by tools/wind_atlas.py and stored in
app/island-wind.json. Licensed CC BY 4.0. The Global Wind Atlas is developed by
DTU Wind Energy in partnership with the World Bank Group, with funding from
ESMAP. https://globalwindatlas.info
The stored monthly values calibrate continuous seasonal functions; the game
generates synthetic weather using smooth seeded disturbances.
https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/temporal/climatology/
The weather functions, wake model and turbine power curves are original
project code. Island outline, zones, firms and grid are fictional; public
Svenska kraftnaet project pages on the Gotland AC connection are references
for the interconnector timing only. No data from them is redistributed.
