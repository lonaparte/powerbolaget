Baltic coastal climate calibration:
NASA POWER, 2001-2020 gridded monthly climatology at 55.6761 N, 12.5683 E.
Parameters WS10M, T2M, ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN.
The stored monthly values calibrate continuous seasonal functions; the game
generates synthetic weather using smooth seeded disturbances.
https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/temporal/climatology/
The climate function and construction scheduler are original project code.
NREL turbine reports, SAM documentation and ORBIT scheduling documentation
are references only; OpenFAST, SAM and ORBIT are not imported or bundled.
